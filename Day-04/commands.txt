---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. 
> whoami — Who am I?

- Shows the current logged-in user.

> whoami-
Example:
rushi
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. id
Shows your user ID and the groups you're a member of.
Example:
uid=1000(rushi) gid=1000(rushi) groups=1000(rushi),27(sudo),1001(developers)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. useradd
- when new developer joins the team and needs a Linux account.
> sudo useradd developer
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ls -l 
 - See ownership & permissions
Example:

-rw-r--r-- 1 rushi developers 1200 script.sh
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. chown
- Change owner
>chown rushi script.sh 
or 
>sudo chown developer file.txt
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. chown user:group 
— Change owner AND group
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.chgrp 
— Change group
>sudo chgrp developers project.txt
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. chmod 
— Change permissions
> chmod 644 file.txt
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
9. Numeric Permission Values
r = 4
w = 2
x = 1

therefore:

7 = 4+2+1 = rwx
6 = 4+2   = rw-
5 = 4+1   = r-x
4 = 4     = r--
0 = ------- 
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10. chmod +x — Make executable
> chmod +x deploy.sh
then: ./deploy.sh

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
11. Symbolic chmod
Instead of numbers, you can use:

>chmod u+x deploy.sh

meaning:
u = user/owner
+x = add execute permission
